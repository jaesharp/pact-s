# PACT-System-Identity Repository (The "Who")

Content to be developed as per the PACT-System multi-repository architecture plan (`../architecture-overview.md`).

This repository is envisioned to manage identity, authentication, and authorization aspects of PACT-System governance.

Key components would include:
- Authentication and authorization systems
- Role-based access controls
- Identity verification processes
- Delegation chains
- Audit logging related to identity and access
- Signature mechanisms for actions and artifacts

Its primary responsibilities include:
- Ensuring only authorized parties can modify governance rules or perform critical PACT-System operations.
- Tracking responsibility for governance decisions and changes.
- Maintaining comprehensive audit trails for access and authorization.
- Providing accountability throughout the governance process.
