# PACT-System-Principles Repository (The "Why")

Content to be developed as per the PACT-System multi-repository architecture plan (`../architecture-overview.md`).

This repository is envisioned to document the foundational principles, ethical considerations, and the overarching rationale behind PACT-System governance decisions and its evolution.

Key components would include:
- Core PACT-System Principles (elaborated, perhaps with historical context or deeper dives than the main `core/principles.md`).
- Decision records and justifications for significant architectural or policy choices.
- The PACT-System vision, long-term roadmap, and strategic goals.
- Ethical frameworks and guidelines applied to or enforced by the PACT-System.
- Impact assessments for major PACT-System versions or policy changes.
- Success metrics and evaluation criteria for the PACT-System's effectiveness in achieving its governance goals.

Its primary responsibilities include:
- Documenting the fundamental rationale behind PACT-System governance rules and structures.
- Ensuring all PACT-System development and operations align with stated ethical principles and core values.
- Providing context and justification for governance decisions made throughout the PACT-System lifecycle.
- Articulating how the PACT-System measures its success and effectiveness.
