# PACT-System-Lifecycle Repository (The "When")

Content to be developed as per the PACT-System multi-repository architecture plan (`../architecture-overview.md`).

This repository is envisioned to manage temporal aspects of PACT-System governance operations and change processes.

Key components would include:
- Temporal scheduling mechanisms.
- Decision timelines and approval workflows.
- Change management protocols for PACT-System rules and versions.
- Transition management for moving between PACT-S Versions (t0, t1, t2).
- Conflict resolution procedures for governance decisions.
- Historical tracking of PACT-System evolution.

Its primary responsibilities include:
- Coordinating the timing of governance activities and verifications.
- Managing the controlled process of transitioning between PACT-S Versions.
- Resolving conflicts that may arise during governance decisions or version updates.
- Maintaining historical records of the PACT-System's lifecycle and evolution.
